Our Story

Born from the belief that
the best gifts are made,
not bought.

"Every family has a raaga. A melody that runs through its stories, its celebrations, its memories. We help you hear it."

RaagaTales was born in Bengaluru from a simple but deeply felt conviction. The most meaningful gifts are not purchased, they are crafted. We started with one question: what if you could give someone the gift of hearing their own story, set to music?

We are a small, passionate studio. Every personalised song we compose and every illustrated story we narrate carries our full attention and care. This is not a service to us. It is a craft. And every order is personal.

Our work draws from the rich tradition of Indian classical music. Its ragas, their emotions, their healing qualities, blended with contemporary production to create something timeless and deeply personal.

What to include in your email
Tell us the occasion, who the gift is for, your preferred language, and any special memories or details you'd like woven in. We'll take it from there and guide you through the rest.
Languages we work in
We create in most Indian languages — Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and more — as well as English. If you have a language in mind, simply mention it and we'll confirm.
Pricing
